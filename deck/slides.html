08Desktop Creator ModeInnovation & interaction · 30%

Everything is a plugin.

Widgets

Ask for a card. Bun drafts it, it runs in a sandbox with a read-only copy of your numbers, and you press Install.

Shell appearance

The theme, what Bun wears, the app’s name. One schema, with Apply, Undo and Reset.

Taint protection

A turn that carries a file, a bill or pasted text cannot change plugins or the look.

Behind your PIN

Creator Mode is off until you turn it on. No plugin can touch money, the PIN or permissions.
