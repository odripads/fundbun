10Security kernel · T0–T4Security & compliance · 30%

The model proposes.
A frozen kernel decides.

Hash-bound PIN approval

The PIN approves one exact action: tool, amount, payee. Change a field and it is void.

Taint propagation

Bills, files, email and pasted text mark the turn untrusted. Risky steps then need your tap, or are blocked.

Hash-chained audit trail

Every decision joins a chain. An edit breaks it, and the app shows where.

Caps and a kill switch

¥500 an action, ¥1,000 a day, ¥5,000 a month. One tap makes Bun read-only.

T4 is never offered

No new payees, outside transfers, investing, credit, or changing its own limits.

FundBun The kernel is six files: security/policy.ts, pin.ts, binding.ts, audit.ts and agent/specs.ts, tools.ts. They are frozen for the final: Creator Mode, attachments and email were added around them, and every new tool passes the same gate. 10 / 15
